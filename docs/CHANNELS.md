# DocForge Publication Channels

## Report

Purpose: structured, evidence-oriented publications.

Common components: navigation, tables, charts, diagrams, callouts, references, appendices, search, print/PDF, and Word export.

## Sales

Purpose: guided customer conversation and decision support.

Common components: situations, assessment choices, decision moments, method visuals, outcomes, seller notes, customer paths, and presentation controls.

## Offer

Purpose: clear commercial scope and decision package.

Required component inventory for future implementation:

- Offer summary and customer context
- Scope and exclusions
- Package options and comparison
- Deliverables and milestones
- Responsibilities
- Pricing table and applicable units
- Commercial assumptions
- Dependencies and prerequisites
- Validity, terms, and conditions
- Approval or next-step block
- Offline print/PDF and Word export

All channels share the same core manifest, navigation, accessibility, asset embedding, offline distribution, and testing contracts.
